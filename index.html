Need help?

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit a maintenance request?

Students living in the South Asian University hostel can create a local student account and submit a request for their block and tower.

What kind of issues can I report?

You can report electrical, plumbing, housekeeping, and other hostel maintenance concerns. Please describe the issue clearly so the helpdesk can respond effectively.

How do I choose my block and tower?

Select Block A or Block B first. The tower list will then update automatically with A1-A5 or B1-B5, depending on the block you selected.

How can I follow the progress of a request?

After signing in as a student, open your dashboard. Your submitted requests show the current status, timeline, and any available completion information.

Where is my account and request information stored?

Hostify is a local demo, so accounts, requests, and feedback are stored in this browser. They do not change the project code or appear on another browser or device.

How does the admin review requests?

The administrator can sign in through the Warden account, view pending and completed requests, update progress, schedule visits, and manage feedback or contact logs.
